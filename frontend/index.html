About Me

Hello! I'm Miriam Lobren, a licensed real estate professional affiliated with CD Miranda Realty. With years of hands-on experience across Las Piñas City, Cavite, and neighboring South Luzon communities, I specialize in helping Filipino families, first-time homebuyers, and Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) find homes that match their lifestyle and long-term financial goals.

From initial model unit tours to securing Pag-IBIG (HDMF) and commercial bank loan approvals, my priority is ensuring a transparent, smooth, and stress-free journey. Every subdivision, townhouse, and villa listed under my portfolio undergoes strict legal verification for 100% clean titles (TCT/CCT) and valid developer Licenses to Sell (LTS).

Whether you're looking for a ready-for-occupancy family home, an affordable pre-selling unit, or a prime investment with strong capital appreciation, I am here to provide transparent price computations, site trippings, and dedicated end-to-end guidance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying, reserving, and financing your dream home with Miriam Lobren & CD Miranda Realty.

We offer flexible financing arrangements tailored to your financial goals, including:

  • Bank Financing: Competitive interest rates with loan terms ranging up to 20–25 years through accredited commercial banks.
  • Pag-IBIG Housing Loan (HDMF): Accessible loan packages with low equity requirements and repayment terms up to 30 years.
  • In-House Financing: Direct developer financing with minimal documentation and expedited approval.
  • Spot Cash / Deferred Cash: Substantial prompt-payment discounts for upfront full cash or short-term installment settlements.

To lock in your selected unit and current pricing, the standard reservation package includes:

  • Two (2) valid government-issued photo IDs (Passport, Driver's License, UMID, etc.).
  • Proof of Income (latest 3 months payslips or Certificate of Employment with compensation).
  • Tax Identification Number (TIN) verification.
  • Proof of Billing address (Meralco, Maynilad, or telecommunications bill).
  • Unit Reservation Fee (applied directly toward your total contract price / equity).
